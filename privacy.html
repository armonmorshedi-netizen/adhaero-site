Adhaero Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Adhaero is a medication adherence app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what the app records, where it is kept, who can see it, and how to remove it. The short version: your records live on your phone; they leave it only when you sign in (to back them up) or when you choose to send them to a physician. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Where it is kept

On your phone, in a store shared with the app's widgets and Watch app. The app works fully without an account.

In your account, if you create one. Signed-in records are backed up to a database hosted by Supabase in the United States (Amazon Web Services, Oregon), protected by row-level security so that only your own sign-in can read your rows. Backups stop when you sign out.

Apple Health data is read only to show it inside the app and is never uploaded to the account backup for advertising, marketing, or sale, and never disclosed to third parties, as Apple's Health rules require. Readings you type into Adhaero (weight, blood pressure, blood glucose) can be written back to Apple Health if you allow it.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • A physician you link to, and only the sections you switch on (for example medications and adherence but not symptoms), and only when you tap Send. A physician cannot read your records directly from the database; they see the package you sent. You can decline or remove a link at any time.
  • Service providers that process data on the app's behalf: Supabase (account and backup database), Google (if you sign in with Google; Google shares your name and e-mail address with Adhaero and nothing else), and Apple (Sign in with Apple, if you use it, which shares your name and an e-mail address or a private relay address; and speech recognition, see below). None of them are allowed to use your data for their own purposes.

Adhaero has no analytics or advertising software and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Voice, camera, and microphone

When you add a medication by voice, audio is transcribed either on your phone or by Apple's speech recognition service, depending on the step and your device. Adhaero does not store recordings. Label and lab-report scans are read on your phone and the photos are not uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Reminder text names the medication; you can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account. Nothing leaves your phone.
  • Export your records as CSV or a PDF report from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings. This removes your account and every backed-up row, including packages sent to physicians and care notes, within minutes. You can also delete the app, which removes everything on the phone.
  • Revoke Apple Health access in the Health app under Sharing.

Children

Adhaero is not directed at children under 13, and no account should be created for a child under 13. A caregiver may keep a child's medications in a profile on their own device.

Medical disclaimer

Adhaero helps you keep track of medications and notice patterns. It does not give medical advice. The adherence score, the modelled medication-level charts shown to physicians, and the correlations between readings and adherence are informational and are not diagnoses. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
