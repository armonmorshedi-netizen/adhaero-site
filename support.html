Adhaero Support

Adhaero keeps your medications on schedule and shows how taking them lines up with how you feel.

Get help

E-mail adhaeroapp@gmail.com. Include the app version from Settings and, if the app crashed, the diagnostics file from Settings > Share diagnostics.

Common questions

Reminders stopped arriving. Open the app once a week; iOS allows a limited number of scheduled reminders, and the app tops them up when it runs. Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings > Adhaero.

Apple Health readings are missing. In the Health app, go to Sharing > Apps and Services > Adhaero and turn on the readings you want to see.

A label scan read the wrong strength. Hold the phone flat over the label in good light and tap the strength to correct it before saving.

Sharing with a physician. Ask your physician for their six-character code or QR code, enter it under Settings > Share with physician, wait for them to accept, then choose the sections to send.

Deleting your account. Settings > Account > Delete account removes your backup and shared records. Deleting the app removes what is on the phone.
